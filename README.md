# Suprpod

A professional networking platform built by a developer, for developers. Canadian-first, with no black-box feed and no pay-to-play visibility.

**Site:** https://suprpod.com (invite only for now)

## What this repo is

This repo has **no source code**. It's the public home for feedback, bug reports, and product updates, so everything about how Suprpod is being built and fixed happens in the open.

## What to expect

- **Bugs:** every report gets an acknowledgement within 24 hours. Issues are triaged weekly, and fixes are credited by name in the Tuesday Product Update (if you gave us your username).
- **Feature requests:** nothing gets built because one person asked. A request moves forward when several people independently want it _and_ it makes Suprpod better at what it's for. Requests we decline get a short, honest reason, not silence.
- **Product Updates:** every Tuesday. What was fixed, what shipped, what's next, and one thing we decided not to do, with the reasoning.

## What Suprpod stands for

These are design constraints, not marketing copy. Feature requests are measured against them.

- **You control your feed.** Chronological, follow-based, or something you configure yourself. No hidden ranking you can't see or change.
- **No engagement bait.** Nothing is optimized to make you angry or keep you scrolling.
- **You're not the product.** Your data isn't sold. It's stored on Canadian servers.
- **Visibility isn't for sale.** If Suprpod ever charges, it will be companies paying for hiring tools, never anyone paying for reach.

## Reporting a good bug

The bug form asks for the page URL, what happened, what you expected, and your browser and device. The more of that you give us, the faster it gets fixed. Screenshots help a lot.

**Please don't paste passwords, invite codes, or private content.** This tracker is public.

## Triaging help

Interested in helping triage bugs? Say so in a Discussion. Contributors who consistently file clear reports may be invited to help label and prioritize.
